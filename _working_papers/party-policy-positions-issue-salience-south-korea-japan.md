This working paper examines whether familiar conservative, progressive, and liberal party labels identify comparable policy positions and parliamentary priorities in South Korea and Japan. It separates parties' documented positions from the issues they discuss, linking a 300-cell position dataset to parliamentary speech records.

The current draft uses fourteen provisionally comparable policy items and speech records from March to July 2026. Its results are conditional on the classification procedure, the selected policy domains, and the available country-specific party samples.
